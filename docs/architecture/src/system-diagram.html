01

How it works

Every email follows the same seven steps. Nothing is searchable until it has passed steps 3 and 4.
Step 1
Mailboxes
  • Every company mailbox is in scope, user and shared; named exceptions are kept out.
  • Access is read-only and granted mailbox by mailbox through Exchange's own permission boundary.
  • The system cannot send, move or change mail.
176 mailboxes today
Step 2
Capture
  • Each email is fetched with its subject, body, participants and attachments, using IDs that survive moves.
  • Attachments are opened and their text extracted; original files are kept.
  • Obvious bulk and automated mail is recorded, not processed.
~19,000 indexed emails a month
Step 3
Protect
  • Masked before storage: government IDs, bank, routing and card numbers, passwords, keys and codes.
  • Health information is blocked entirely and never indexed.
  • Text is screened for hidden instructions; email content is always treated as untrusted.
In-tenant services, no prompt rules
Step 4
Understand
  • The AI answers 21 fixed questions per email: action required, approval, invoice, project risk, urgency, category.
  • It reads out properties, entities, people and facts as written, never inferred.
  • A second check asks whose action item it is.
Labels are proposals, not approvals
Step 5
Store
  • Text is cut into passages and turned into meaning vectors for search by idea, not just keyword.
  • Everything lives in PostgreSQL in the Finishes Azure tenant; files in private Blob storage.
  • Copies of one email in several mailboxes are linked, never merged.
One database, one audit trail
Step 6
Ask, as yourself
  • You ask from Claude, Copilot or ChatGPT after a Microsoft 365 sign-in.
  • The assistant reaches mail only through one authorized API. It never holds a database key.
  • Your identity decides what you can see, on every question.
Search · threads · action items · risks
Step 7
Get the evidence
  • Every answer cites sender, date, subject and mailbox and links to the real email and its files.
  • An empty result means "not found in mail you may read", never a guess.
  • Links open a read-only page under the same access rule.
Admin console for review, costs, people
02

How it gets smarter

Two loops, both corrected by people, never approved by the AI.
A
A knowledge base learned from the mail itself

Properties, companies, people, terms, meetings and how they relate are extracted from each email and checked against its text. A name seen in two or more messages is handed back to the AI as context for the next one, so references get resolved the way the company actually writes them. No seeded list, no approval queue.

B
Reviewer answers become examples

In Label Review, a person marks each AI label right or wrong. Corrected emails are shown to the AI as worked examples on later calls, corrections first, then mail from the same sender domain. Precision and recall per label come from the same answers.

C
Risk picture accumulates

Each project-risk email gets an impact and likelihood rating with a stated reason. Over time this builds the risk matrix, the trend by category and the property heatmap.

D
Thresholds move only by hand

The system suggests where a label's yes/no line should sit. A person changes it. Nothing retunes itself silently, so behaviour is explainable at any date.

03

How it stays accurate

Rules that are enforced, and results that are measured, not asserted.

Enforced on every email and every question

Masking is a service, not an instruction

Sensitive numbers are found by deterministic patterns and an in-tenant detection service before the AI ever reads the text. A masking outage holds the email rather than letting it through.

Authorization runs inside the database

Who is asking is checked in SQL before any ranking. Per-reader limits (a person, a domain, a term) only narrow results and apply to every tool and link alike.

Email text is untrusted input

Instructions found inside a message are ignored by the classifier and by every assistant. Injection attempts are screened and flagged.

Every result is traceable

Sender, date, subject, mailbox and a link to the original. Summaries and labels are shown as AI proposals. Duplicates are collapsed only after authorization.

Nothing is silently dropped

Every email ends in a recorded state: published, held with a reason code, not indexed, or failed. Operators see counts, backlog and failures per run.

Every call is audited

Who asked, which tool, which emails came back. Query text is not logged by default. Access changes are written to the audit trail as well as to Entra.

Measured separately, with counts
Capture completeness
per mailbox

Mail found in the mailbox versus mail captured. Misses are counted, not assumed away.

Processing success
per run

Published, held by reason, failed and dead, plus the age of the oldest waiting email.

Label quality
precision · recall

Per label and per person, from Label Review answers, always with the sample size. A probability is not an accuracy.

Retrieval quality
recall@5 · @10

Against a question set with known answers, including questions that should return nothing.

Authorization negatives
must be zero

Wrong audience, missing role, revoked grant, excluded mailbox, paused mode, forged filter: each must return no results.

Accuracy is a measured, published number with a sample size, never a single percentage. Thresholds are uncalibrated until a labelled sample says otherwise, and the console shows that status.
